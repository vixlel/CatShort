Cat Shorts

Cat Shorts is a personal desktop tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It puts together short cat videos from footage the owner is allowed to use (their own clips, clips shared with the creator's permission, and openly licensed stock footage) and, once the owner approves them, uploads them as YouTube Shorts to the owner's own YouTube channel.

To do this it asks for one Google permission: uploading videos to the signed-in user's YouTube channel.
