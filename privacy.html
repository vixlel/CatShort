Cat Shorts Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What the app accesses

Cat Shorts asks for the YouTube upload permission so it can upload videos that its owner has approved to the owner's own YouTube channel. It does not request or read your email, contacts, or any other Google data.

How data is stored and used

The sign-in token that Google issues is stored only in a file on the owner's own computer and is used only to upload videos. Cat Shorts has no server of its own. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party, and there is no analytics or advertising.

Sharing

Google user data is never sold, shared, or transferred to anyone.

Limited Use

Cat Shorts's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the app's data folder from your computer.

Contact

Use the support email shown on the Cat Shorts Google sign-in screen.
